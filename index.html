Wayfinding

Your Route to the Lab

Step 1Locate Dauterive Hall on campus

Head to 635 Downey Way on USC's University Park Campus. Dauterive Hall (VPD) is the brick building on the south edge of campus, between Childs Way and Exposition Boulevard, just west of USC Pardee Way. USC Gould School of Law sits between VPD and Exposition Blvd to the southwest, and Lewis Hall (USC Price) is right behind it to the northeast.

Closest parking: Flower Street Parking Structure, across Figueroa at Exposition
Metro E Line: Expo Park/USC station, then walk east on Exposition Blvd toward Pardee Way

Step 2Enter the building

Walk in through either entrance. Once inside, you'll be in the ground floor atrium, a tall, open lobby. The lab is one level below you on the Lower Level (LL). Look for the stairs or elevator.

Step 3Go down one floor

Use the central staircase or the elevator to go down one level. From the stairs, turn left into the hallway. The elevator opens onto the same hallway from the other side. Each lab door has a room sign and a doorbell camera beside it.

Elevator available for accessibility

Step 4Find your suite

Check your SONA confirmation email; it tells you which suite to go to. Each door has a sign with the room number. If you're unsure, just call us.

  • BUAD 307 (MKT) → LL-102 Computer Lab
  • BUAD 304 / 497 (MOR) → LL-112, LL-110, or LL-102, depending on the study
  • Paid studies → the suite varies by study

For MOR and paid studies, your SONA confirmation names the suite.

At the door: Every lab door is card-access only. Ring the doorbell camera next to the door and wait; a Research Assistant will see you and come let you in. Doors are not left open, even during sessions. Outside building hours, the researcher running your session meets you at the building entrance.

Lab Spaces

Which Suite Am I Going To?

Every door is card-access only

Every lab door is card-access only. Ring the doorbell camera next to the door and wait; a Research Assistant will see you and come let you in. If no one has come after a minute or two, call 213.821.9752.

LL-102 Computer Lab

BUAD 307 (MKT) BUAD 304 / 497 (MOR)
Left side of the Lower Level hallway. 16 computer stations for online survey studies.
Card-access door. Ring the doorbell camera and wait; an RA will let you in and show you to your computer station.

LL-112 Lab Suite

BUAD 304 / 497 (MOR)
Past the central stairs, entrance on the left. Multi-room suite for behavioral experiments and group studies.
Card-access door. Ring the doorbell camera at the suite entrance and wait; an RA will let you in and take you to your study room.

LL-110 Lab Suite

BUAD 304 / 497 (MOR)
Past the central stairs, entrance on the right. Multi-room suite with specialized research equipment and a control room.
Card-access door. Ring the doorbell camera at the suite entrance and wait; an RA will let you in and take you to your room.

Lab Manager Office · LL-110-A
